### 1. What new improvements were introduced in Gemini 3.0?
Gemini 3.0 the most intelligent model with state-of-the-art(most advanced) reasoning and multi modal capabilities(interact with multiple data type). it brings capabilities of previous gemini's all models together help you solve your most complex problems. 

### 2. How does Gemini 3.0 improve coding & automation workflows?
Gemini 3.0 improves coding by generating more accurate code, debugging better, and understanding multiple files at once. It also has stronger function-calling and integrates smoothly with tools like the Gemini CLI, making automation and development faster and easier.

### 3. How does Gemini 3.0 improve multimodal understanding?
Gemini 3.0 has stonger multi modal capabilities meaning it has much stronger ability to process and combine multiple datatypes (text, images, audio, video). it can understand visuals more accurately like reading charts, documentation, screenshots etc.
